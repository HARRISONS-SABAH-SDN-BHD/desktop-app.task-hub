Harrison ExcelFlow report integration:
- Open ExcelFlow from Task Hub after selecting its application folder.
- Choose a completed ExcelFlow run from an email routine, filter by branch/type/version, and import selected reports.
- Reports become verified fixed attachment copies. New ExcelFlow runs do not silently change saved routine attachments.
- Running/failed runs, missing or changed files, unsafe paths and duplicate filenames are rejected.
- Existing email scheduling and calendar behavior are preserved.
